Questions

Which AI can I connect?

ChatGPT and Claude, plus other AI apps that can add a custom MCP connector with sign-in, and tools like Claude Code, Codex and Cursor. You can connect up to three AIs per account. The Connect page has the steps. Each AI service has its own access requirements, terms and privacy policy.

Can I use it before connecting my AI?

Yes. Pick a routine and start with cards for the part of the day you’re in. Connect your AI whenever you like, and it plans each day for you. A subscription is required.

How much is it, and is there a free trial?

US$9.99 a year in the United States. That’s the launch price for our first 1,000 subscribers, and it’s yours as long as you stay subscribed. Eligible new subscribers get a 7-day free trial, as determined by the App Store. The App Store shows your local price, eligibility and renewal terms. There is one plan, yearly, and no free tier. After the trial, paid features and AI connections need a subscription. Your existing data stays readable without one.

How do I cancel?

Cancel in your iPhone’s Settings under Subscriptions, at least 24 hours before the trial or the period ends, to avoid renewal. Apple handles purchases, renewals, cancellation and refund requests. Deleting your one-by.one account does not cancel an Apple subscription.

How is my data handled, and what does my AI receive?

The operator opens stored content only to fix a problem you report, keep the service secure or meet a legal obligation. Text you write (card titles, notes, interests, memories, calendar event names) is stored encrypted with a key specific to your account. When you delete your account, we delete that key. Backups made before deletion can still contain it until they expire; after that the deleted text can no longer be read. When your connected AI reads your plan or records, it receives what its tools return, such as card titles, results and memories. Disconnecting blocks future access. What it already received is kept or deleted under that AI service’s policies.
