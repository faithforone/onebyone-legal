Sam makes one-by.one.

He has ADHD and plans his own days with it: his AI writes the plan, and his iPhone shows one card at a time.

“I built one-by.one because opening my to-do list had become a task of its own.”

Sam writes the posts on the blog. Prices, plans and features are checked against each company’s official pages, and every post shows the date it was last updated. Quotes from Reddit are short, link to the thread and give the month they were posted. Each post also says where one-by.one is not the better pick.

Why it exists is in the manifesto. To plan with your own AI, start on the Connect page.
