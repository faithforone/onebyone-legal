Claude Calendar Integration: Google, Apple and Outlook Calendar

By one-by.one · Updated November 21, 2026

Claude calendar integration comes in three official forms: the Google Calendar connector, the Microsoft 365 connector for Outlook, and Calendar access in the Claude iPhone app for Apple Calendar. Anthropic’s Help Center lists all three for every plan, Free included, and each reads your events.

Claude Google Calendar integration

  1. In Claude, open Customize, then Connectors, and select +.
  2. Find Google Calendar and select Connect.
  3. Sign in to Google and grant the permissions.
  4. In a chat, select +, then Connectors, and turn Google Calendar on.

Claude can then view shared calendars, create, update and delete events, find mutual availability and answer invitations. On Team and Enterprise plans, an Owner enables the connector first.

Claude Apple Calendar integration: iPhone, iCloud and Mac

The Claude iPhone app works with the iPhone’s Calendar app on all Claude plans. Claude reads your calendar, finds free slots and adds events: “When am I free this week for a 30-minute meeting?”

On a Mac, Fantastical 4.1.10 and later has a connector for the Claude desktop app that reads, creates, edits and deletes events. For an iCloud calendar, add the iCloud account to Fantastical itself.

Claude Outlook calendar integration

The Microsoft 365 connector is on all Claude plans, with a personal Microsoft account on Free, Pro, Max and Ultra. A work or school account needs one-time consent from a Microsoft Entra Global Administrator. It is read-only until write tools are enabled; then Claude creates, updates and deletes events.

Claude Notion Calendar integration, Calendly and Reclaim

Notion Calendar shows the Google, iCloud and Outlook accounts you connect to it, so connect those calendars to Claude. Claude’s directory also lists Calendly and Reclaim.ai.

Claude Google Calendar integration not working

  1. Turn Google Calendar on in the chat.
  2. Open Customize > Connectors, select the Google connector and Disconnect, then connect again.
  3. Still failing: at myaccount.google.com/connections, choose “Delete all connections” for Claude and reconnect.
  4. Work account: a Workspace admin sets Claude as Trusted. It takes about 15 minutes.

In August 2026 a Reddit user saw every Calendar call fail with “This connector requires additional permissions.” A later reply: “The Calendar connector works again.”

Claude calendar MCP

  • google-calendar-mcp: several Google accounts at once.
  • mcp-server-apple-events: Apple Calendar on macOS.
  • caldav-mcp: iCloud, Fastmail and Nextcloud.

A remote MCP server goes in as a custom connector, and Free users are limited to one. Steps: how to add a custom connector in Claude.
