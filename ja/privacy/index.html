one-by.one Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 5, 2026

This policy covers the one-by.one website, iPhone app and MCP connection service, operated by salad76, a sole proprietorship in the Republic of Korea represented by Seungeun Kim, doing business as one-by.one. one-by.one is an execution layer for the AI service you already use. Your connected AI prepares plans; one-by.one presents their actions as cards and keeps execution records that the AI can read with your permission. one-by.one does not itself call an AI model.

Starter cards are supplied in the app, not generated by an AI. Without an AI connection, each routine you pick becomes one starter card. Creating these cards happens on your device and does not send data outside it. Cards you add this way are saved and synced to your account like any other card.

The launch scope is worldwide except the countries and territories listed in our terms. The interface is English-only.

Launch waitlist

If you ask to be notified at launch, we store your email address, signup time and signup page, plus an email hash to prevent duplicate signups. We use this to send one launch email, with no further marketing unless you opt in later. We also use a hashed IP address to limit abusive submissions; these rate-limit records become eligible for cleanup after two days of inactivity.

Waitlist entries become eligible for cleanup 30 days after the launch notice, or when unsubscribed. To remove your signup before then, email privacy@one-by.one.

What we collect

  • Account: account ID, email when available, provider identity and sessions, to sign you in and link your records. Apple sign-in requests email. Google sign-in requests openid email profile. Google identity and access tokens are sent to Supabase Auth for sign-in. The app keeps its session in device-only Keychain storage; web authorization uses browser session storage.
  • Plans and results: full task titles, notes, steps, dates, time zones, estimates, targets, measurements, progress, execution records, corrections and versions. These show your day plan and what to do now, record results and support synchronization.
  • Interests and memories: titles, planning notes, memory text, user reports, AI summaries and inferences, sources and timestamps. These provide saved planning context. Corrections and withdrawals retain originals.
  • Connections and operation: device and AI connection IDs, credential references, hashed server credentials, permissions, approvals, expiry, revocation, synchronization records, write requests and receipts, request-limit records and local startup/sign-in diagnostics. These control access and support retries and operation. To limit abusive subscription-linking requests, we store a SHA-256 hash derived from the request’s IP address, a rate-limit window timestamp and an attempt count. This rate-limit table does not store the raw IP address. Network providers may separately process IP addresses to deliver and protect requests.
  • Support: messages you email us and account-linked bug descriptions, submission IDs, hashes, received times and selected app version, iOS version or error code. Bug-report tools add no automatic attachments.
  • Subscription: Apple handles payments. We receive and store subscription product and transaction identifiers, purchase-to-account associations, purchase claim tokens, verification status, environment, and relevant purchase, expiry and grace-period dates. These records let us verify access and link an eligible purchase made before sign-in to a one-by.one account. We do not receive your payment-card details.
  • Deletion processing: Account deletion can leave a limited deletion receipt containing the deletion request identifier, hashes of the account identifier and recovery capability, and Apple revocation status and job reference. An Apple revocation job can retain encrypted authorization material and the Apple account subject needed to retry revocation, together with retry and status metadata. These records are described under Retention.

If you sign in with Google, our sign-in provider stores what Google shares for that sign-in: your email address and whether Google verified it, your name, your profile picture link and your Google account identifier.

Text you enter may contain health, work, location or other sensitive details. Share only what you want stored and available to your connected AI.

The app can schedule local reminders for running timed cards. Delivery depends on your notification settings, quiet hours and the state last known to this iPhone. The current app does not register for remote push.

For App Store subscription prices, introductory offers and renewal terms, see our terms and Apple’s purchase confirmation.

Purposes and grounds for processing

  • Account, synchronization and AI connections: we process account identity, sessions, saved plans, results, interests, memories and approved connection permissions as needed to provide the service you request. AI access is limited to the permissions you approve; connecting an AI is optional. Storage consent and the separate optional AI and calendar choices are described below.
  • Subscription verification: we process transaction references and entitlement status to provide paid access and meet applicable purchase-record obligations. Apple handles payment details.
  • Support and privacy requests: we use the messages and diagnostics you choose to send to answer your request; we process privacy requests to meet applicable legal obligations. Please avoid including health or other sensitive information that is unnecessary for your request.
  • Security and service operation: we use access, request-limit and technical records as needed to protect accounts, prevent abuse and operate the service, subject to applicable law and any consent it requires.
  • Launch waitlist: your voluntary signup authorizes one launch email. You can withdraw by unsubscribing or emailing us; rate-limit records are used only to prevent abusive submissions.

Saving and syncing records is a condition of using one-by.one’s recording and execution features. Records can include meals, exercise, quantities and other health-related information. The current service requires storage consent for record changes and for connected AI apps to read account records; it does not offer a separate non-health recording mode.

You may decline or withdraw storage consent. New record changes and uploads are then blocked, and connected AI apps cannot read your records. Existing records are not deleted by withdrawal. Declining during initial setup stops setup; you can go back, sign out and manage an existing Apple subscription. Connecting an AI app and sharing calendar data are separate, optional choices. Storage consent alone does not authorize an AI connection. Declining or withdrawing consent does not cancel an Apple subscription.

Consumer health data and explicit consent

Meal and intake records, exercise or fitness activity, weight, medication and other health-related measurements, notes, results or AI inferences may reveal physical or mental health. We treat these as sensitive health-related data. They come from information or measurements you choose to record and from your approved AI's reports or inferences based on that content.

We use this data only to save your requested records, synchronize them, show progress and provide the planning or changes you request. Supabase hosts and processes it; Cloudflare carries authorized AI requests and replies; your chosen AI provider receives it when you separately consent to sharing and approve the relevant connection permissions. These are the recipients and categories of recipients for these uses. If you put health information in a support email, our email provider also receives it as described under International transfers.

Saving and syncing health-related records is subject to the storage consent condition above. Each AI connection and calendar sharing require separate approval.

You can withdraw storage consent in the app's Settings or email privacy@one-by.one. Withdrawal stops future collection or sharing covered by that consent when the service receives and confirms the request; an offline request must reconnect to reach the server. Withdrawal does not itself delete existing records or copies already received by an AI provider. You can separately request deletion, including retained originals and history, using the rights procedure below. Withdrawal does not affect processing already lawfully carried out.

We do not sell consumer health data or use it for targeted advertising. Washington's My Health My Data Act and Nevada's SB 370 provide consumer health-data protections that may apply even when other state privacy laws do not. See Major regional privacy rights for access, withdrawal, deletion and appeal options.

Calendar (optional)

Calendar sharing is optional and off until you consent and choose calendars. You can withdraw in Settings by turning off calendar sharing, or email privacy@one-by.one for help.

If you enable calendar sharing and choose calendars, one-by.one uploads a snapshot covering the next seven days. It contains event titles, start and end times, all-day status, tentative status where available, hashed event and calendar identifiers, and snapshot metadata such as capture time, time zone, coverage dates and whether the snapshot is incomplete.

On your iPhone, calendar access also reads calendar identifiers and names, event metadata and your attendance or availability status to select the events to share. Event locations can be read by the local calendar reader but are excluded from the uploaded snapshot. Calendar notes, attendee details, locations and URLs are not uploaded. one-by.one does not edit your calendar events.

With Share times only enabled, uploaded event titles are replaced with “Busy.” Previously uploaded titles are removed when the server confirms withdrawal of the earlier snapshot. Information already received by an AI provider is not automatically removed.

We keep only the latest uploaded calendar snapshot for your account and refresh it when you return to the app, at most once every 15 minutes unless you change your calendar selection or the times-only option.

Calendar data remains available to connected AI until the server confirms withdrawal. Offline withdrawal is retried when the app reconnects. Deleting your account also deletes its uploaded calendar snapshot.

What your AI app receives

When an AI connection you have approved calls an MCP tool, one-by.one returns the account data allowed by that connection’s permissions. Depending on the request, this can include saved interests and planning notes; plans and action cards; running, waiting and completed activity; targets, quantities and recorded results; corrections and retained originals; saved memories and their sources; and calendar snapshots you separately chose to share. Replies can also contain identifiers, versions, access permissions, write handles and operation receipts.

Each connection requires its own approval. context:read allows relevant planning context, activity:read allows activity records, and activity:write allows supported changes. The approval page shows the requested access. MCP tool replies do not expose sign-in credentials, provider tokens or device secrets. User-written content can itself contain identifying or sensitive information.

ChatGPT, Claude and other connected AI services are operated by their respective providers. Their policies, account type and settings govern the copies they receive, including retention and any use to improve their services. one-by.one does not control those independent copies. Disconnecting stops future access through that connection; it does not automatically delete previously received information.

OAuth account-information requests are separate from MCP record replies. Review the OAuth scopes displayed on the approval screen.

Write instructions require your explicit request. The server checks granted permissions but cannot prove what you agreed to in chat. Some changes cannot be undone; corrections can preserve originals.

What connected tools can do

Available tools vary with the account and supported features. They can read planning context and records, create or change plans, record requested execution changes and results, manage saved interests and memories, and submit an approved support report. A tool response can be paginated or incomplete and is not necessarily a complete lifetime history.

Read plans and tasks
get_planning_context, list_current_work, get_current_work: read planning context, task lists and task/result records.
Plan days and edit unstarted tasks
open_or_plan_day, edit_day_plan, replace_plan: open or plan days, change queued cards and replace unstarted plans.
Start and correct work
start_work, undo_start, correct_work_time: start a task, undo an eligible mistaken start, or amend or withdraw a recorded time while retaining its original. Time correction does not control execution.
Save and correct results
record_past_result, correct_recorded_result, complete_work: record separate past work, correct or withdraw a result's interpretation, or mark work complete.
Read interests and memories
list_interests, get_interest: read saved interests, context and memories, including retained originals.
Save and change interests and memories
create_interest, replace_interest_context, save_interest_memory, correct_interest_memory: save interests and memories, replace context or correct or withdraw a memory while retaining its original.
Connection and support
get_connection_info: read permissions and supported features, and receive a write token when available. submit_bug_report: send an approved report to one-by.one support and receive a receipt.

Prompt links and clipboard

When you choose Copy and open, one-by.one copies the displayed prompt to the system clipboard and opens the selected AI service. For ChatGPT and Claude, the prompt is also included in the opening URL. Opening that URL can disclose the prompt to the service before you press Send in the chat. Prompts can contain the onboarding answers and planning details used to prepare them.

Copy only places the text on the clipboard without opening an AI link. Other apps receive it if you paste or otherwise share it through your device’s clipboard features. Gemini currently opens without a prompt in its URL, while the text remains available on the clipboard. These actions are separate from approving access to saved records through MCP.

Sharing images

If you share it, the app or recipient you select receives that image under its own policies. Review the final image before sharing. Automatic keyword warnings or exclusions cannot identify every sensitive item.

The service processes readable content to provide synchronization and authorized tool replies; it does not provide end-to-end encryption that prevents the service from reading that content. App sessions are stored in device-only Keychain storage.

Retention

Account content is retained while the account exists. Corrections and withdrawals of activity interpretations can preserve originals and history; they are not privacy deletion requests. Confirmed account deletion removes account-owned content from the live database, subject to the limited records below.

  • Deletion receipts expire 30 days after creation. Expired receipts are removed when the deletion-cleanup job runs.
  • Apple revocation jobs become eligible for deletion once they are more than 30 days old and no retained deletion receipt references them. Encrypted authorization material is cleared when revocation processing completes; unsuccessful jobs can retain it until cleanup.
  • Unlinked Apple purchase notifications expire seven days after receipt and become eligible for cleanup. Expired notifications are not used to link purchases.
  • Subscription-claim IP-hash records with a rate-limit window older than one day are deleted when a subsequent claim request runs the cleanup. This is not a guaranteed 24-hour deletion deadline.
  • Bug reports become eligible for cleanup after 180 days and account-linked reports are deleted earlier with the account.
  • Calendar snapshots become eligible for cleanup after their shared window has been over for more than one day.
  • AI write receipts have a 24-hour replay window; device write receipts have a seven-day replay window. Expired receipts can remain while needed for pending corrections or active synchronization.
  • Waitlist entries become eligible for cleanup 30 days after the launch notice, or when unsubscribed. Waitlist IP-hash rate-limit records become eligible after two days of inactivity.

Expiry or cleanup eligibility is not the same as completed deletion.

Deletion and disconnection

To delete your account in the app, open the menu, tap Delete account below Sign out and confirm. If you cannot reach the account menu, including during setup, email privacy@one-by.one for deletion assistance.

When the server confirms account deletion, the sign-in account and its account-owned plans, results, interests, memories, calendar snapshot, connections, device records and bug reports are removed from the live database. There is no account-restoration grace period. Limited deletion receipts, Apple revocation jobs, independent rate-limit records and later unlinked Apple purchase notifications can remain for the purposes and periods described under Retention.

After server confirmation, the app removes this account’s local records, session, device credentials and notifications from this iPhone. A failed local cleanup requires a retry. Account deletion does not cancel an Apple subscription or automatically erase AI chats, support email, shared images, exported files, other-device copies or operating-system backups.

Apple authorization revocation is attempted where applicable.

Manage independently held copies through the relevant provider, or contact us for help.

The current app creates no private CloudKit backup. If earlier app data appears in iCloud, manage it in iOS Settings › Apple Account › iCloud › Manage Storage. Account deletion does not remove separate CloudKit copies or synchronizing backup keys; deleting iCloud app data does not establish that those keys are removed.

Disconnecting an AI app revokes future tool access. It does not erase records or copies that app already received.
