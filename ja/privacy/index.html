one-by.one Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 5, 2026

This policy covers the one-by.one website, iPhone app and MCP connection service, provided by salad76, a sole proprietorship in the Republic of Korea represented by Seungeun Kim, doing business as one-by.one. The launch scope is worldwide except the countries and territories listed in our terms. The interface is English-only.

Launch waitlist

If you ask to be notified at launch, we store your email address, signup time and signup page, plus an email hash to prevent duplicate signups. We use this to send one launch email, with no further marketing unless you opt in later. We also use a hashed IP address to limit abusive submissions; these rate-limit records expire after two days of inactivity.

We keep your signup until the launch notice is sent, then delete it after 30 days. If you unsubscribe, it is eligible for deletion immediately by our scheduled cleanup. To remove your signup before then, email privacy@one-by.one.

What we collect

  • Account: account ID, email when available, provider identity and sessions, to sign you in and link your records. Apple sign-in requests email. Google sign-in requests openid email profile. Google identity and access tokens are sent to Supabase Auth for sign-in. The app keeps its session in device-only Keychain storage; web authorization uses browser session storage.
  • Plans and results: full task titles, notes, steps, dates, time zones, estimates, targets, measurements, progress, execution records, corrections and versions. These show your day plan and what to do now, record results and support synchronization.
  • Interests and memories: titles, planning notes, memory text, user reports, AI summaries and inferences, sources and timestamps. These provide saved planning context. Corrections and withdrawals retain originals.
  • Connections and operation: device and AI connection IDs, credential references, hashed server credentials, permissions, approvals, expiry, revocation, synchronization records, write requests and receipts, request-limit records and local startup/sign-in diagnostics. These control access and support retries and operation.
  • Support: messages you email us and account-linked bug descriptions, submission IDs, hashes, received times and selected app version, iOS version or error code. Bug-report tools add no automatic attachments.
  • Subscription: Apple handles the paid, auto-renewing App Store subscription. We store subscription product identifiers, transaction references, the app-account association, environment, status and relevant expiry or grace-period dates to verify access. We do not receive payment-card details.

If you sign in with Google, our sign-in provider stores what Google shares for that sign-in: your email address and whether Google verified it, your name, your profile picture link and your Google account identifier.

Text you enter may contain health, work, location or other sensitive details. Share only what you want stored and available to your connected AI.

The app can schedule local reminders for running timed cards. Delivery depends on your notification settings, quiet hours and the state last known to this iPhone. The current app does not register for remote push.

Plans: $12.99 per month or $59.99 per year in the United States; the App Store shows the price for your country. A 14-day introductory free trial is available to eligible subscribers, as determined by the App Store. The purchase screen shows eligibility, the applicable price and renewal terms. If you do not cancel at least 24 hours before the trial or a period ends, the subscription renews and your Apple Account is charged. After the trial, a subscription is needed for paid product features and AI connections. Existing data can still be read, and account deletion, disconnection and sharing withdrawal remain available without a subscription.

Purposes and grounds for processing

  • Account, synchronization and AI connections: we process account identity, sessions, saved plans, results, interests, memories and approved connection permissions as needed to provide the service you request. AI access is limited to the permissions you approve; connecting an AI is optional. Where applicable law requires consent, we obtain it. Health-related data and calendar sharing require the separate express choices described below.
  • Health-related data and calendar sharing: we rely on your explicit consent for these optional uses, including collection and sharing with your chosen AI. These choices are separate from accepting the terms or signing in.
  • Subscription verification: we process transaction references and entitlement status to provide paid access and meet applicable purchase-record obligations. Apple handles payment details.
  • Support and privacy requests: we use the messages and diagnostics you choose to send to answer your request; we process privacy requests to meet applicable legal obligations. Please avoid including health or other sensitive information that is unnecessary for your request.
  • Security and service operation: we use access, request-limit and technical records as needed to protect accounts, prevent abuse and operate the service, subject to applicable law and any consent it requires.
  • Launch waitlist: your voluntary signup authorizes one launch email. You can withdraw by unsubscribing or emailing us; rate-limit records are used only to prevent abusive submissions.

Consumer health data and explicit consent

Meal and intake records, exercise or fitness activity, weight, medication and other health-related measurements, notes, results or AI inferences may reveal physical or mental health. We treat these as sensitive health-related data. They come from information or measurements you choose to record and from your approved AI's reports or inferences based on that content.

We use this data only to save your requested records, synchronize them, show progress and provide the planning or changes you request. Supabase hosts and processes it; Cloudflare carries authorized AI requests and replies; your chosen AI provider receives it when you separately consent to sharing and approve the relevant connection permissions. These are the recipients and categories of recipients for these uses. If you put health information in a support email, our email provider also receives it as described under International transfers.

The app's consent screen asks for explicit consent to collect and use health-related data and, separately, to share it with your chosen AI. General AI permissions or accepting these terms do not replace health-data consent. Declining these choices leaves the corresponding health-data recording or sharing features unavailable; you can still use features that do not require that data.

You can withdraw health-data consent in the app's Settings or email privacy@one-by.one. Withdrawal stops future collection or sharing covered by that consent when the service receives and confirms the request; an offline request must reconnect to reach the server. Withdrawal does not itself delete existing records or copies already received by an AI provider. You can separately request deletion, including retained originals and history, using the rights procedure below. Withdrawal does not affect processing already lawfully carried out.

We do not sell consumer health data or use it for targeted advertising. Washington's My Health My Data Act and Nevada's SB 370 provide consumer health-data protections that may apply even when other state privacy laws do not. See Major regional privacy rights for access, withdrawal, deletion and appeal options.

Calendar (optional)

Calendar sharing requires explicit consent on the app's consent screen and is off until you consent and choose calendars to share. You can withdraw that consent in Settings by turning off calendar sharing, or email privacy@one-by.one for help. When you turn it on, one-by.one uploads the next seven days of event titles, start and end times, and all-day status from the calendars you choose, together with hashed event and calendar identifiers. These events are shared with your connected AI to help it plan around your fixed commitments.

You can turn on Share times only in the calendar screen. Your AI sees “Busy” instead of event names; real event names stay on your iPhone. Titles are shared by default when you choose calendars. Changing this option requests withdrawal of the previous snapshot and uploads a fresh replacement.

We do not upload calendar notes, attendees, locations, or URLs, and one-by.one does not write to your calendars. We keep only the latest uploaded calendar snapshot for your account and refresh it when you return to the app, at most once every 15 minutes unless you change your calendar selection or the times-only option.

Calendar data remains available to connected AI until the server confirms withdrawal. Offline withdrawal is retried when the app reconnects. Deleting your account also deletes its uploaded calendar snapshot.

What your AI app receives

When tools read your records, full saved task, result and memory text is sent to your chosen AI client. Replies can include targets, measurements, retained originals, sources, IDs, versions, permissions, write tokens and receipts.

OAuth authenticates your identity. one-by.one separately records the product permissions you approve: context:read, activity:read, and activity:write. Approvals are requested and recorded on the consent page. These control planning context, activity reads and changes. Tools do not return your sign-in email or personal profile, provider tokens, push tokens or device secrets. Text you put in a task, memory or report can still contain those details.

ChatGPT is provided by OpenAI; Claude by Anthropic. The AI client's handling of chats and tool data is governed by that provider's policy and settings. Check those before connecting.

Write instructions require your explicit request. The server checks granted permissions but cannot prove what you agreed to in chat. Some changes cannot be undone; corrections can preserve originals.

Operator access and encryption

We don't routinely look at your content. Operator access to stored data is limited to fixing a problem you report, keeping the service secure, or meeting a legal obligation. Text you write (card titles, notes, interests, memories, calendar event names) is stored encrypted with a key specific to your account. When you delete your account we delete that key; database backups taken before deletion can still contain it until those backups expire, after which the deleted text can no longer be read.

Retention

Account content is kept while the account exists. Corrections preserve originals and history; a correction or withdrawal of an activity record is not a privacy deletion request. Deleting the account removes its content from the live database immediately. Server backup copies, including any account encryption key in a backup made before deletion, are retained for a maximum of 30 days and then expire; this can leave backup copies for up to 30 days after account deletion.

We keep bug reports for 180 days, then remove them from the live database through scheduled cleanup; account-linked reports are deleted earlier with the account.

Calendar snapshots are eligible for scheduled cleanup once their shared window ended more than one day ago. AI write receipts have a 24-hour replay window; device write receipts have a 7-day replay window. Expired replay receipts are removed by bounded scheduled cleanup, except while needed for a pending correction or a live synchronization scope. Facts, results, corrections and history are retained with the account. Account-linked technical records are also deleted with the account.

We keep unlinked purchase notifications (original transaction identifier, notification identifier, type and receipt time) to reconcile a purchase with its account. This also applies when Apple sends a notification after account deletion. These notifications expire seven days after receipt and become eligible for bounded scheduled cleanup; duplicate deliveries while a receipt is stored do not extend its expiry. Expired notifications are not used to link a purchase or restore account content. Linked notification records are removed with the account.

Deletion and disconnection

To delete your account in the app, open the menu, tap Delete account below Sign out and confirm. For deletion help, email privacy@one-by.one.

Accepted account deletion removes the sign-in account and its linked plans, results, interests, memories, connections, device records, technical records and bug reports in a database transaction. There is no grace period.

The app's deletion cleanup removes its local one-by.one records, session, that account's device credentials and notifications on this iPhone after server confirmation. Cleanup can fail and require a retry. Apple authorization revocation is attempted where applicable.

Deletion does not erase exported files, Calendar events, legacy app data, other-device or operating-system backup copies, support email or AI chats. Manage independently held copies through the relevant provider, or contact us for help. Account deletion does not cancel an Apple subscription.

The current app creates no private CloudKit backup. If earlier app data appears in iCloud, manage it in iOS Settings › Apple Account › iCloud › Manage Storage. Account deletion does not remove separate CloudKit copies or synchronizing backup keys; deleting iCloud app data does not establish that those keys are removed.

Disconnecting an AI app revokes future tool access. It does not erase records or copies that app already received.
