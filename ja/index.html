Here’s to the overwhelmed.

The overthinkers.
The overplanners.
The tab hoarders.

The ones with 47 things marked “important.”
The ones who make a list of their lists.
The ones who know exactly what they should be doing.

And still can’t start.

The ones who’ve tried every habit tracker.
Losing a whole Sunday to a system
that saves them ten minutes on Monday.

For the ones still asking:

What should I do now?

This. Just this one.
