Questions

Is one-by.one made for ADHD?

It’s a planner for anyone who freezes in front of a long list, and it was made by someone with ADHD. It doesn’t diagnose or treat anything.

Do I have to plan the day myself?

No. Tell ChatGPT or Claude what’s on, in a sentence. It orders the day, adds travel and prep, and sends the cards to your iPhone.

Which AI can I connect?

ChatGPT and Claude, plus other AI apps that can add a custom MCP connector with sign-in. The Connect page has the steps.

Can I start before connecting an AI?

Yes. Pick a short routine in the app and start with that. Connect your AI whenever you like. A subscription is required.

How much is it, and is there a free trial?
