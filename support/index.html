one-by.one Support

Last updated: October 4, 2026

Get help

Email support@one-by.one. Tell us what you tried, what happened and any error message. App and iOS versions can help. Do not send passwords, sign-in tokens or payment-card numbers.

Email to support@one-by.one and privacy@one-by.one is forwarded to the owner. Only the owner reads it. We do not promise a response time.

We keep support and privacy email for up to 2 years, then delete it. You can ask us to delete your messages sooner.

Bug reports sent through your connected AI are linked to your account. They contain the description and diagnostics you agree to share, with no automatic attachments. A receipt means stored; it does not promise a reply or fix.

Connect ChatGPT or Claude

Sign in with Apple or Google. Email sign-in is for existing accounts; the app does not offer email account creation.

In the app, open Settings › AI apps and follow the connection guide. In ChatGPT or Claude, search for one-by.one in its apps or connectors directory. Sign in to one-by.one and review the requested access before approving it.

If you need a custom connector address, use https://mcp.one-by.one/mcp. Ask the AI explicitly to save or change a plan, memory or result. Read what it receives before connecting.

Disconnect an AI app

Use Disconnect on the connection's one-by.one authorization page. You can also remove the connector through your AI app's controls. The native AI apps list does not itself revoke access.

Revoking access blocks future tool calls. It does not delete your records or copies already received by the AI app.

Delete your account

To delete your account in the app, open the menu, tap Delete account below Sign out and confirm. For deletion help, email privacy@one-by.one.

Account deletion removes account content, connections and bug reports from the live database immediately when accepted. Database backups roll off within 30 days.

Deletion does not erase exported files, Calendar events, copies in AI chats or other independently held copies. Manage them through the relevant app or provider. See deletion details.

Subscription help

Purchases, renewals, cancellation and refunds are handled through Apple. Account deletion does not cancel your auto-renewing subscription; cancel it separately through Apple.
