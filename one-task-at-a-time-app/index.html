Questions

Why one task at a time instead of a list?

A list shows everything and asks you to choose. A card shows one thing and asks you to start. The choosing is done once, by your AI, when it plans the day.

Can I still see what’s coming?

Yes. The rest of the day is in the app, and Later puts a card off. The main screen stays on the one to do now.

Which AI can I connect?

ChatGPT and Claude, plus other AI apps that can add a custom MCP connector with sign-in. The Connect page has the steps.

Can I start before connecting an AI?

Yes. Pick a short routine in the app and start with that. Connect your AI whenever you like. A subscription is required.
